Bacterial Coinfections among Patients Hospitalized with Respiratory Viral Illness

Sarah Mathew

2026-11-04

Introduction

The dataset is collected by Georgia EIP surveillance conducted by the RESP-NET team. The dataset includes hospitalizations due to Influenza, COVID-19, and RSV infections. Data were obtained through full chart abstractions and includine information on race/ethnicity, sex, age, and positive bacterial cultures.

The objective of the analysis is to characterize bacterial coinfections among patients with viral respiratory illness. Specifically, this analysis aims to:

  • Determine and describe the interactions between viral and bacterial pathogens among bacterial coinfections.
  • Determine and compare the proportions of bacterial coinfections among respiratory viral hospitalizations across viral pathogens (Influenza, COVID-19, and RSV).
